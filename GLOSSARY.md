# Glossary

- **Additionality:** outcomes caused by an intervention beyond the counterfactual.
- **Attribution:** the share of an observed change credited to the intervention rather than others.
- **DALY:** disability-adjusted life year; years of life lost plus years lived with disability.
- **Deadweight:** outcomes that would have happened without the intervention.
- **Displacement:** benefit offset by harm or reduced activity elsewhere.
- **HDI:** Human Development Index, combining health, education, and income indices.
- **IHDI:** inequality-adjusted HDI.
- **KPI:** key performance indicator.
- **NPS:** Net Promoter Score; promoter percentage minus detractor percentage.
- **QALY:** quality-adjusted life year.
- **SROI:** social return on investment; present social impact divided by inputs.
- **VSL:** value of a statistical life, inferred from many small wage-risk trade-offs.
- **WELLBY:** one-point life-satisfaction improvement sustained for one year.
